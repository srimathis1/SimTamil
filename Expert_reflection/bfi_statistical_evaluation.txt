3. METRIC INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------
MAE:
Average absolute difference between AI and human Big Five scores.

MSE:
Average squared prediction error. Larger errors receive greater penalty.

RMSE:
Square root of MSE, expressed on the original 1-5 Big Five score scale.

Pearson Correlation:
Measures the linear association between the human and AI Big Five profiles.


4. FORMULAS
------------------------------------------------------------
Absolute Error = |AI Score - Human Score|

MAE = Sum(|AI Score - Human Score|) / Number of Traits

MSE = Sum((AI Score - Human Score)^2) / Number of Traits

RMSE = sqrt(MSE)

Pearson r = Covariance(Human, AI) /
            (SD_Human × SD_AI)


5. EVALUATION METHODOLOGY
------------------------------------------------------------
Human ground truth: BFI-44 Wave-1 responses.
AI prediction: Big Five scores generated from the participant transcript and Wave-1 data.
Reverse-coded BFI items are scored using 6 - raw response.
Each Big Five trait is calculated as the mean of its corresponding BFI items.
All reported evaluation metrics are calculated statistically; no LLM judge is used.

Note: Pearson correlation here is descriptive because this Participant-01 analysis contains only five aggregate trait observations. A larger participant sample or item-level predictions provides a stronger basis for correlation-based inference.
